Privacy Policy

Tech Studio YouTube Uploader · Last updated: 21 November 2026

This privacy policy covers the "Tech Studio YouTube Uploader" app (Google Cloud project "yutu"), a private tool operated by Sharad (Dev Marlow) of Tech Studio. It is used only to manage the operator's own YouTube channels.

What the app does

The app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions, and schedule publishing on YouTube channels owned by the operator. It is not offered to the public and is not used to access anyone else's account.

Data accessed

When an account owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the app can access that account's YouTube channel information and the videos, playlists, captions and thumbnails it manages. The app does not request or read email contents, contacts or other Google account data.

How data is stored

The OAuth access token is stored only on the operator's own computer, so the app can act on the operator's channels. No YouTube or Google user data is sent to, stored on, or shared with any server operated by Tech Studio or any third party.

Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to anyone. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also complies with the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the locally stored token file also removes the app's access.
